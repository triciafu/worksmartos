Privacy Policy

Overview

WorkSmartOS is a pre-launch software product focused on AI-powered workforce automation. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

Information We Collect

When you submit the enterprise launch form, we may collect your name, work email, company, role, team size, automation interest, comments, submission source, and submission date.

How We Use Information

We use the information you submit to manage the WorkSmartOS enterprise launch list, understand interest in our product, contact you about updates or early access opportunities, and improve our product planning.

How We Store Information

Form submissions are stored in a Google Sheet controlled by WorkSmartOS. We aim to collect only the information we need for launch communications and product planning.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers, such as Google, to store or process information needed to operate this website and launch list.

Your Choices

You can ask to update or remove your information, or opt out of launch communications, by emailing us at contact@worksmartos.com.

Future Product Updates

As WorkSmartOS develops accounts, integrations, AI features, email tools, billing, or enterprise services, we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect those features.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@worksmartos.com.
